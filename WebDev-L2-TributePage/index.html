01 — HIS STORY

From a small town
to the stars.

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

Raised in a modest family, he developed a deep curiosity about the world and a lifelong commitment to learning. He studied aeronautical engineering and became one of India's best-known aerospace scientists.

His work with India's space and missile programmes earned him national recognition. In 2002, he became the 11th President of India, serving until 2007. Many remember him most warmly as a teacher who encouraged young people to dream boldly and work with purpose.

02 — THE JOURNEY

Milestones that mattered.

Each chapter carried the same belief: knowledge becomes powerful when it serves people.

1931

A curious beginning

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, where a lifelong love of learning began.

1960s

Into aerospace

Joined India's scientific community and contributed to the country's growing space programme.

1980

A satellite milestone

As project director, helped lead the SLV-III mission that placed the Rohini satellite in orbit.

2002

The People's President

Became India's 11th President, connecting with citizens and young people across the country.

2015

Teaching until the end

Passed away while speaking to students at IIM Shillong.
